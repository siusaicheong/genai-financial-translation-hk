# Online Appendix: Example 11 - LLM Term Extraction

## Discussion

An LLM-based method was then applied to the same pair of paragraphs. Owing to its ability to model context and understand the meaning of entire passages, the LLM produced a more comprehensive list of bilingual terms and multi-word expressions. It correctly identified longer phrases (for example, "principle of keeping intervention into the way in which the market operates to a minimum" and its Chinese equivalent) and captured a wider range of relevant terms. This shows that LLMs offer a more intelligent approach to bilingual term extraction and help produce higher-quality glossaries.
